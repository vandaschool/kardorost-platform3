# Kardorost Next

A fresh Laravel 13 / PostgreSQL / Vue 3 / Inertia 3 implementation of Kardorost.

Implemented modules include secure accounts and scoped panels, course enrollment and capacity, learning rooms and private submissions, organization reports, installment billing, transfer receipts, consultation scheduling and gateway verification, a private manual refund workflow, and organization seat quotas with atomic group enrollment and scoped CSV exports. Organization membership now supports seven-day phone invitations, explicit acceptance after phone verification, and private UTF-8 CSV previews for up to 100 rows. Invalid rows block confirmation; normalized duplicates, existing members and existing invitations are skipped. Preview confirmation is creator-scoped, expires after 15 minutes and is idempotent. Neither bulk import nor the legacy add-member endpoint creates immediate membership or provisions accounts. Invitations use internal notifications and can be discovered after normal registration; invitation SMS is optional through a durable database outbox. Melipayamak REST and console pattern adapters support immediate OTP submission and queued organization invitations. Each invitation revision permits one provider attempt; duplicate workers cannot resend it, ambiguous outcomes remain in review, and interrupted attempts are reconciled without automatic resend. Provider acceptance is distinct from handset delivery. Real credentials/templates and live delivery remain unverified; delivery receipts and consultation reminders are incomplete. Sponsored seats share cohort capacity with personal reservations and do not create personal tuition invoices. Internal contract invoices now support immutable issuance, full or partial gateway and independently reviewed transfer payments with a cumulative allocation ledger, and scoped organization access. Original invoice totals stay fixed; late or concurrent funds exceeding the remaining balance are retained in review without allocation. Current organization managers can request full manual refunds of captured unallocated corporate funds. Admin review and private transfer evidence are separate steps, bank details are encrypted, and former payers have no personal access to organization evidence. Refunds preserve invoice allocations and balances. Allocated-fund refunds, credit transfers between invoices, dated organization installment schedules, formal tax/accounting integration and automatic license purchases remain incomplete.

Consultation entitlements can now be issued by admins against a reviewed source reference, scoped to one student, coach, exact session duration and expiry date. Students explicitly select a grant for direct or human-coordinated bookings. Each confirmed session consumes a ledger entry; cancellation before the start releases it once, while completion and no-show retain consumption. Booking keeps the listed tariff but creates no separate payment. Existing confirmed sessions survive grant suspension or expiry changes. Manual grant issuance does not record a purchase. Paid personal course purchases can now include frozen consultation terms per enrollment billing version. Admins explicitly choose activation after the first allocated payment or full settlement; verified gateway payments, approved transfer receipts and recovered payments share one issuance path. Replays and concurrent repairs cannot duplicate or extend grants. Unavailable participants block benefit issuance while preserving paid tuition and active enrollment, with an admin repair queue. Free, sponsored and pre-existing purchases do not receive retroactive benefits. Standalone consultation packages now have versioned admin offers, explicit student acceptance, frozen orders, dedicated verified Zibal callbacks and independently reviewed private transfer receipts. Fulfillment issues exactly one grant linked to the settled payment. Late or duplicate funds remain in review; admins can fulfill eligible frozen orders or use the existing full manual refund workflow for captured unallocated funds. Payment purpose guards isolate package funds from course, single-session and organization settlement. Sponsored consultation bundles, cancellation/refunds for already issued package grants and benefit reversal following active-course cancellation remain incomplete.

The assessment infrastructure supports immutable publications, resumable encrypted answers and versioned reports for five-option subscale sums with reversed items. Real instruments and scoring keys will be selected later; no questionnaire content is seeded into the application. Other instrument types, paid assessments, career reports and AI interpretation remain incomplete.

See [the Persian installation and delivery guide](README-FA.md) for setup, completed functionality, remaining modules, verification results, and integration limitations. This repository contains incremental working modules, not the completed platform.
